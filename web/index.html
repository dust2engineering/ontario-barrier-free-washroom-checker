What changed in the 2024 edition

The 2024 Code rewrote Subsection 3.8.3: there is no longer a fixed 1,500 × 1,500 mm stall size (turning-space and clearance rules replaced it), the stall door clear opening rose from 810 to 850 mm, the seat height range moved from 400–460 to 430–485 mm, and the universal washroom turning space grew from 1,500 to 1,700 mm diameter. A washroom that complied in 2012 can fail in 2024.
